## RenCon 2025 repository for results

This repository contains the results for the RenCon 2025. The audio and MIDI files for audition round and final round are stored in the respective folders. Additionally we kept team's report, unless the team asked to kept their methodology private. The rendering systems (code, scripts) are also not released in this repository.

The results for online audition round and live final round are kept in the csv files.
